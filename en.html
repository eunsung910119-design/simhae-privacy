심해 원정대 Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-06
Developer: the 심해 원정대 developer
Contact: GitHub issues

1. Information we collect

"심해 원정대" (the "App") does not collect any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, and no data is sent to a developer server. The game can be played offline; the App connects to Google Play only for in-app purchases and purchase restoration.

2. Information stored on your device

Game progress (save data) and display settings are stored only in the App's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot access. They are not sent to the developer and are removed when you uninstall the App.

If Android backup (Google account backup) is turned on for your device, this data may be included in your device backup and kept in your Google account. That backup is managed by Google under your account; the developer cannot see it. You can turn off or delete backups in your device's backup settings.

3. In-app purchases

The App sells the following three non-consumable items (one-time purchases that stay with you) through Google Play Billing. None of them are randomized (loot box) items.

  • Idle Boost — increases the efficiency and duration of rewards earned while you are away.
  • Auto Merge — automatically merges gear up to a rarity you choose.
  • Big Barracks — raises the companion roster limit from 30 to 100.

Payment details (card, account, etc.) are handled by Google; the developer does not receive them. To apply and restore your purchases, the App only checks your purchase status with Google Play on your device and does not send the result to the developer. For refunds and support, the developer can only see order numbers and purchase status in Google Play Console. Google's Privacy Policy applies to payment processing.

4. Third parties, ads, analytics

The App shows no ads, uses no analytics or crash-reporting tools, and shares no data with third parties.

5. Children

The App is not primarily directed at children under 13. The App does not ask for age or collect personal information.

6. Your rights

Because no personal information is collected, there is nothing to access or delete on the developer's side. Data on your device can be removed by uninstalling the App or via Settings > Apps > 심해 원정대 > Storage > Clear data.

7. Changes

Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, please contact GitHub issues.
